About

I like putting the motors somewhere smarter.

I'm Justice Hickman-Maynard, an engineering student at Harvard who designs robots and the mechanisms inside them. I'm the chief engineer of the Harvard Undergraduate Robotics Club, where I've led the mechanical design of two generations of our Mars rover, and a course assistant for ES 51, Harvard's introductory mechanical engineering course.

I've also built research hardware for the Harvard Microrobotics Lab and spent summer 2026 re-engineering a production research arm so it could be manufactured at scale. I got my start in FRC with Team 125, the NUTRONs.

The problems I enjoy most are moving mass away from the end of an arm, designing out backlash, and turning unusual kinematics into hardware that actually runs. I work across the whole loop: concept and kinematics, CAD, analysis, machining and fabrication, then wiring, firmware and test.
