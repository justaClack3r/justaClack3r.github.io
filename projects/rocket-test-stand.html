Context

The Harvard Rocket Propulsion Group, part of Harvard SEDS, develops liquid bipropellant rocket engines. Liquid engines are static-fired on a test stand long before they fly. The stand has to hold the engine and its plumbing rigidly, carry the full thrust into the ground, and survive the exhaust firing after firing.

Design

I designed the stand for an engine of 800 lbf thrust. It is a tall vertical frame built mostly from aluminum to keep it light and easy to work on. The engine sits vertically inside the frame, and its thrust goes into a mounting plate and down the longerons to the base. Where the exhaust hits, a steel plate takes the heat and blast that aluminum wouldn't survive.

Validation

I validated the frame with a SolidWorks static study on the longeron model, applying the 800 lbf design thrust at the engine mount and checking the factor-of-safety distribution through the structure.
