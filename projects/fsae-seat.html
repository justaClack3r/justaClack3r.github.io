FSAE Driver Seat Surface Model

A surface-modeling project: a Formula SAE driver seat shaped in SolidWorks to meet the competition rules, with a plan for building a carbon-fiber seat from it.

The seat is a freeform surface model, built from boundary and lofted surfaces rather than prismatic features, and shaped to meet the Formula SAE competition requirements.

Manufacturing plan:

  1. Slice the surface model into a stack of strips.
  2. Hot-wire-cut each strip from foam.
  3. Stack and fair the strips into a smooth, full-size foam form of the seat.
  4. Use the foam form as the mold for curing a carbon-fiber seat.
