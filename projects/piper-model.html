Piper Seminole RC Model

In high school I modeled a twin-engine RC airplane in SolidWorks, based on the Piper PA-44 Seminole.

The Piper PA-44 Seminole is a twin-engine light training aircraft with a T-tail. My model follows its layout: a T-tail, two wing-mounted nacelles and a smooth lofted fuselage with a canopy, with the control surfaces broken out as separate parts.

The flight was unsuccessful. The model still looks great, and it was my first real lesson that looking right in CAD and flying right are two different problems.
